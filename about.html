About E2I

A brighter future for every young person.

Who We are

Educate to impact (E2I) is a non-profit organisation that simplifies access of vulnerable youth from high school to college and from college to work.We link vulnerable young people to TVETs and universities, and to the employment and enterprise Opportunities,so they can build a dignified livelihood.

Target Group

Vulnerable youth, meaning young people facing Financial, access or opportunity barriers to education, skills and work.

Approach

Guide, Place, Follow up, Support.

Tagline

Leaving No Youth Behind.

Key Message

The gap is not education. The gap is transition.

Our Vision & Mission

Vision

A world where every young person has the opportunity to learn, work and thrive.

Mission

To link vulnerable youth to education, skills and work opportunities through partnerships, support and innovation.

Together, We Can Do More.

Every contribution, partnership and act of support helps us create real opportunities for vulnerable youth.
