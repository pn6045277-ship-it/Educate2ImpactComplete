Who we are

Educate 2 Impact

Educate 2 Impact (E2I) is a non-profit organization based in Laikipia County, Kenya, expanding access to skilled trades training and work opportunities for young people facing financial barriers. We partner with quality TVET institutions to finance training for vulnerable youth, particularly girls and young people from low-income and rural communities, and connect them to employment and entrepreneurship opportunities. By bridging the gap between training and the world of work, we help young people build pathways to dignified livelihoods and brighter futures.

Why we exist

Finishing school should be the beginning of opportunity, not the end of the journey.

Across Kenya, many young people complete secondary school but struggle to transition to college, skills training, and meaningful work. Financial barriers keep some from accessing further training, while limited career guidance and connections to employment opportunities leave others uncertain about their next steps.

Educate 2 Impact exists to bridge this gap. We help young people facing financial barriers access skilled trades training and connect them to employment and entrepreneurship opportunities, creating pathways from school to skills, and from skills to dignified livelihoods.

The Transition Fund

Study Now. Pay It Forward.

The Transition Fund is a revolving fund designed to help young people access short, market-relevant courses and support youth-led microenterprises. The principle is simple: you receive support to train. Once employed and earning an income, you contribute an agreed, affordable share to the fund, helping the next generation of young people access the same opportunities.

How it works

1

Access training

We help eligible youth overcome financial barriers to short courses lasting 2–6 months.

2

Transition to work

We connect young people to employment and entrepreneurship opportunities.

3

Pay it forward

Once earning an income, beneficiaries contribute an agreed, affordable share to help finance opportunities for future learners.
